# The People's Government: Investigation Into Federal Privatization, Private Control, and the Transfer of Public Authority

**Case classification:** Pre-Litigation Investigation
**Status:** Evidence Gathering / Legal Review Required
**Jurisdiction:** United States — Federal
**Opened:** November 18, 2026
**Public page:** `/cases/federal-privatization-public-authority`

> No complaint has been filed. This is not an active lawsuit. Nothing in these materials is a finding that any agency, official, or company acted unlawfully.

## Defining question

When the government contracts with private interests, who ultimately controls the public function, and who remains accountable to the people?

## Status labels used throughout

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Verified | Confirmed against a primary or reliable public record cited in the register |
| Under investigation | Records requested or being reviewed |
| Disputed | A credible source contradicts the claim |
| Unverified | A lead or allegation with no confirming record yet |

## Relationship to the Cordova 2028 initiative

Cordova 2028 has published a separate prospective policy framework (the Federal Public Authority, Privatization Transparency, and Accountability Initiative). The two efforts may share verified primary-source research. Neither effort treats the other's policy conclusions as evidence of wrongdoing.
