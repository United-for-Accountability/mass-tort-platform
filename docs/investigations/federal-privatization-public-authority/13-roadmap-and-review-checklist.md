## Section 2: Publication and legal-review checklist

Before publishing or updating the public case page, verify each item:

### Factual accuracy

- [ ] Every factual claim is tied to a specific record in the evidence register
- [ ] Every record is authenticated and cited with its source, date, and URL
- [ ] Verified facts are labeled Verified
- [ ] Unverified claims are labeled Unverified
- [ ] Disputed claims are labeled Disputed with the basis for the dispute
- [ ] Legally unresolved matters are labeled Legally unresolved
- [ ] No implemented change is described without evidence of implementation
- [ ] No allegation is stated as fact
- [ ] No speculation is stated as established

### Legal accuracy

- [ ] No statement asserts that any agency, official, or company acted unlawfully without proven legal violation
- [ ] Legal analysis accurately reflects current precedent (including FCC v. Consumers' Research, 2025)
- [ ] Counterarguments are stated fairly and completely
- [ ] The page correctly notes that FOIA is the most likely near-term pathway
- [ ] The legal notice section is present and accurate
- [ ] No attorney-client relationship is implied
- [ ] The Ninth Amendment is not stated as an independent cause of action

### Source and attribution

- [ ] All sources are cited with their full title, issuer, date, and URL
- [ ] No source is mischaracterized
- [ ] News sources are described as corroboration, not proof
- [ ] Public submissions are described as leads, not evidence

### Status labels

- [ ] Every row in the chronology has an accurate status label
- [ ] Every allegation section entry has a status label
- [ ] No Unverified item has been treated as Verified

### Privacy and security

- [ ] No submission content has been published without the submitter's public-use permission
- [ ] No submitter's contact information is in the published version
- [ ] No classified or restricted information is in the published version
- [ ] No credentials or access tokens are in the published version

### Attorney review

- [ ] An independent attorney has reviewed the legal analysis sections
- [ ] Any attorney recommendations have been incorporated or noted as disputed
- [ ] The attorney's review date is recorded in the investigation file

## Correction protocol

When a factual error is identified on the public page:
1. Correct the error immediately.
2. Add a Corrected label to the corrected item with the date.
3. Add an entry to the evidence register noting the correction and the basis for it.
4. Do not delete the original statement — record what was corrected and why.
5. Update the README with the correction date.
