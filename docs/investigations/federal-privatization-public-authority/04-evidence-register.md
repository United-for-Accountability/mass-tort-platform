## E-001 — $50.1 Billion Funding Request

**Issuer:** U.S. Department of Transportation, OMB, FAA
**Title:** Joint Letter Regarding Additional ATC Funding
**Date:** November 17, 2026
**URL:** https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2026-11/Joint%20Letter%20Regarding%20Additional%20ATC%20Funding.pdf
**What it shows:** A joint request to the House and Senate Appropriations Committees for $50.1 billion for air traffic control modernization and airport infrastructure. Breaks down spending including up to 140 FAA-owned towers, common automation platform, communication infrastructure.
**What it does not show:** Who will own new software; data rights provisions; contractor decision authority under the integrator contract.
**Status: Verified**

## E-002 — Project 2025 Mandate for Leadership, Ch. 19

**Issuer:** The Heritage Foundation
**Title:** Mandate for Leadership: The Conservative Promise, Chapter 19 (Department of Transportation), pp. 632–634
**Date:** April 2023
**URL:** https://archive.org/details/2025_MandateForLeadership_FULL
**What it shows:** Policy proposals to separate the FAA from DOT (or the ATO from the FAA), fund ATC through user fees, operate the FAA more like a business. Describes these as requiring legislation.
**What it does not show:** Any implementing action; any evidence proposals have been carried out; any government endorsement.
**Status: Verified**

## E-003 — FAA Nationwide Ground Stop Q&A

**Issuer:** FAA
**Title:** Nationwide Ground Stop: Questions and Answers
**Date:** November 15, 2026
**URL:** https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/nationwide-ground-stop-questions-and-answers
**What it shows:** FAA's preliminary explanation of the November 13, 2026 nationwide ground stop: legacy equipment became unstable when data flooded the FAA Telecommunications Infrastructure network, affecting 17 of 22 en route centers. Root-cause investigation ongoing.
**What it does not show:** Root cause determination; whether contractor action contributed; whether modernization program related; whether any design or maintenance decision preceded the failure.
**Status: Verified**

## E-019 — FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Exemption

**Issuer:** Ballard Spahr (legal alert reporting on FinCEN action)
**Title:** FinCEN exempts U.S. entities and persons from beneficial ownership reporting
**Date:** August 24, 2026
**URL:** https://www.ballardspahr.com/insights/alerts-and-articles/2026/08/fincen-permanently-exempts-us-entities-and-persons-from-beneficial-ownership-reporting
**What it shows:** U.S. companies and U.S. persons are permanently exempt from Corporate Transparency Act beneficial ownership reporting as of August 2026. This limits one avenue for identifying contractor ownership structures.
**What it does not show:** Foreign ownership; beneficial ownership under other disclosure regimes (SEC, state corporate registrations, private equity disclosures).
**Status: Verified**
