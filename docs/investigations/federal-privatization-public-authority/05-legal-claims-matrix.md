## How to read this matrix

Each row describes a potential legal theory. For each theory, the matrix records:
- The legal basis
- What must be proven for the claim to succeed
- What the current record shows
- Whether the claim is currently ripe
- What additional evidence is needed

## Claims not pursued in this investigation

The following are noted only to explain why they are not being pursued:

- **Ninth Amendment as independent cause of action.** Courts have not treated the Ninth Amendment as an independent source of enforceable rights or standing. No court has sustained a Ninth Amendment cause of action independent of a recognized right. This investigation does not pursue a Ninth Amendment theory.
- **Procurement protest.** Available only to actual or prospective bidders. This organization is not a bidder and cannot file a protest.
- **Qui tam under the False Claims Act.** Requires evidence of a specific false claim submitted to the government. No such evidence has been identified.
