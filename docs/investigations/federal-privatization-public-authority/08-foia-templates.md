## Priority 1: Peraton prime integrator contract

**Agency:** Federal Aviation Administration, Office of Acquisition and Business Services
**Portal:** https://www.faa.gov/foia
**Suggested subject line:** FOIA Request — FAA Brand New Air Traffic Control System Prime Integration Contract with Peraton

**Request text:**

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request the following records:

1. The contract between the Federal Aviation Administration and Peraton Inc. (or any predecessor or affiliate) for the Brand New Air Traffic Control System prime integration services, executed approximately December 2025, including:
   a. The base contract document and all exhibits and attachments;
   b. All modifications to the contract issued through the date of this request;
   c. The statement of work or performance work statement;
   d. Any quality assurance surveillance plan;
   e. Any approved or pending organizational conflict-of-interest mitigation plan.

2. Any FAA approval, authorization, or delegation of authority document associated with this contract that specifies what decisions the contractor is authorized to make on behalf of the FAA, or what decisions require FAA approval.

3. Any data rights, software license, or government purpose rights provisions attached to or incorporated into the contract.

If portions of the records are exempt, please provide the non-exempt portions with a Vaughn index identifying the specific exemption for each withheld portion.

I am willing to pay reasonable search and duplication fees up to $[amount]. If fees will exceed this amount, please notify me before proceeding.

This request is made in connection with a public-interest investigation. I request a fee waiver on the grounds that disclosure is likely to contribute significantly to public understanding of the operations or activities of the government and is not primarily in the commercial interest of the requester.

## Priority 4: Restructuring and privatization studies

**Agency:** Federal Aviation Administration; Department of Transportation; Office of Management and Budget
**Suggested subject line:** FOIA Request — Studies or Reviews of FAA Organizational Restructuring, Privatization, or User-Fee-Based Funding

**Request text:**

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request the following records from [date range: January 1, 2025 through the date of this request]:

1. Any study, analysis, review, memorandum, or briefing prepared by or for the FAA, DOT, or OMB regarding:
   a. Separation of the FAA or the Air Traffic Organization from the Department of Transportation;
   b. Privatization of air traffic control services or infrastructure;
   c. User-fee-based funding for air traffic control;
   d. Transfer of operational control of any air traffic system to a non-federal entity;
   e. Organizational restructuring of the FAA or ATO.

2. Any communications between FAA or DOT officials and outside organizations regarding any of the above topics.

If responsive records are voluminous, please prioritize records from January 1, 2026 through the date of this request.

## Filing guidance

1. Each request should be filed separately with the appropriate agency FOIA office.
2. Keep a copy of every request with the date filed.
3. Track response deadlines (20 business days for initial response; 10 additional days for unusual circumstances).
4. If no response is received, send a follow-up after the statutory deadline.
5. If records are improperly withheld, an administrative appeal is available before judicial review.
6. Consult 05-legal-claims-matrix.md for the legal framework for FOIA claims if records are denied.
