## Cross-reference protocol

When a record is relevant to more than one case or investigation:
1. The record is cited in the evidence register of each relevant investigation independently.
2. Each investigation reaches its own conclusions based on its own record.
3. No inference or finding from one investigation is imported into another without independent evidentiary support.
