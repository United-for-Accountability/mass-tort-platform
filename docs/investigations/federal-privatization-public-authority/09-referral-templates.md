# Inspector General and Congressional Referral Templates

**Investigation:** Federal Privatization, Private Control, and the Transfer of Public Authority
**Date:** November 18, 2026

---

> Referrals to oversight bodies are appropriate only when specific, documented evidence of possible waste, fraud, abuse, or violation of law has been identified. These templates are placeholders. They should not be used unless the evidence record warrants a referral.

---

## When referrals are appropriate

A referral to an Inspector General is appropriate when the investigation has identified:
- Specific evidence suggesting a violation of law, regulation, or policy
- Specific evidence suggesting waste, fraud, or abuse
- Specific information the IG is not likely to already have

A referral to Congress is appropriate when:
- The evidence record is sufficiently developed to support specific, accurate factual claims
- The matter falls within a committee's jurisdiction
- The referral advances accountability rather than merely advocacy

As of November 18, 2026, neither condition has been met. These templates are for future use.

## Template 1: Inspector General referral

**To:** [Agency] Office of Inspector General
**Re:** Referral of Information Regarding [specific issue]

The undersigned submits the following information for the Office of Inspector General's consideration.

**Summary of concern:**
[One paragraph describing the specific concern, the specific agency program or contract involved, and the specific evidence that supports the concern.]

**Supporting information:**
[List the specific records, with citations, that support the concern. Each record should be authenticated and cited to a primary source.]

**What we are not alleging:**
This referral does not allege that any specific individual has committed a crime or violated the law. It identifies specific information that may warrant the IG's review under its authority to examine waste, fraud, and abuse.

**Contact information:**
[Name and contact information for follow-up.]

## Template 2: Congressional oversight referral

**To:** [Committee name and address]
**Re:** Request for Oversight Review — [subject]

We respectfully request the Committee's oversight attention to the following matter.

**Background:**
[Brief description of the federal program or agency at issue.]

**Specific concern:**
[Specific, factually supported concern, with cited records.]

**Questions we believe the record raises:**
1. [Specific question the Committee might ask the agency.]
2. [Specific question the Committee might ask the contractor.]

**Records we have identified:**
[List of records with citations.]

**Records the Committee may wish to request:**
[List of records not yet obtained that the Committee has authority to request.]

We are available to provide additional information or to meet with Committee staff.
